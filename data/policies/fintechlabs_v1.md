# FinTechLabs Global Transport Policy

## 1. General Eligibility
All permanent full-time personnel and retained long-term contractors residing within a 45 km radius of the FinTechLabs technology park are eligible for company-sponsored shared transport. Contractors receive identical routing priority to full-time staff.

## 2. Shift Schedules and Cut-Off Timings
General office shifts operate on flexible morning windows between 08:00 and 11:00, with evening departures between 17:00 and 22:00. The cut-off deadline for roster changes, pickups, or drop modifications is 18:00 on the day of travel.

## 3. Night Shift Transport and Security Escort
Night transport services operate between 21:00 and 06:00. Security escorts are mandatory only for drops occurring after 22:00 (10:00 PM) for female employees. Drops scheduled between 20:00 and 22:00 do not include a dedicated security escort unless specific high-risk zones are flagged by Global Security.

## 4. Weekend and Holiday Travel
Weekend cab services are permitted exclusively for On-Call Engineering and Live Production Support teams. To secure weekend cab access, the employee must obtain written Director-level approval 48 hours prior to the requested weekend shift. Standard development staff are not eligible for weekend transport.

## 5. Airport and Ad-Hoc Travel Approvals
Airport transportation can be scheduled directly through the MoveInSync portal with Direct Line Manager approval. Requests must be submitted at least 4 hours before flight departure. Approval from Department Heads or VPs is not required for domestic business travel.

## 6. No-Show and Cancellation Penalties
Cancellations must be logged at least 60 minutes prior to driver arrival. A first unexcused no-show will incur a mandatory ₹500 deduction from the employee's monthly transport allowance or payroll. Accumulating 3 no-shows within a quarter will result in permanent revocation of company cab privileges.
