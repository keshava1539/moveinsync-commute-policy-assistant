# HealthTech Solutions Transport Guidelines

## 1. General Eligibility
Transportation services are accessible to all verified laboratory technicians, clinical research analysts, and administrative employees residing within a 30 km radius of the HealthTech Medical Innovation Hub. Third-party vendor personnel are excluded from door-to-door services.

## 2. Shift Schedules and Cut-Off Timings
Due to critical clinical trial monitoring schedules, shift handovers occur strictly at 06:00, 14:00, and 22:00. The roster modification cut-off is strictly enforced at 14:00 on the prior working day to allow route safety audits.

## 3. Night Shift Transport and Security Escort
For all female healthcare and support workers departing the campus after 21:00 (9:00 PM), a trained corporate security marshal is assigned to each shared cab. The marshal remains in the vehicle until the final staff member is securely dropped at their residence.

## 4. Weekend and Holiday Travel
Employees rostered for weekend hospital-support and emergency diagnostic shifts are eligible for weekend shuttle services. No special manager approval is needed; inclusion in the hospital department duty roster automatically authorizes weekend cab bookings.

## 5. Airport and Ad-Hoc Travel Approvals
Airport transportation is strictly NOT managed or provided by the MoveInSync transport desk. Employees traveling for conferences or clinical site inspections must arrange personal travel and submit commercial cab invoices directly to the Finance department for reimbursement.

## 6. No-Show and Cancellation Penalties
Cancellations must be filed at least 3 hours prior to the shift start. A second no-show within any 60-day period triggers a formal written warning from People Operations and a 7-day administrative freeze on transport bookings.
